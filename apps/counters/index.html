Raid-counter methodology
  • Each counter is represented by six identical attackers at the selected level with 15/15/15 IVs.
  • Up to 12 evenly sampled ordinary boss fast/charged combinations are simulated three times; bosses with 12 or fewer use every combination.
  • Boss move selectors restrict those combinations. Unselected moves are equally weighted across the tested combinations, including in counter breakdowns.
  • Open a counter to run 32 detailed trials per tested boss moveset. Time on field and boss hits survived are averaged over Pokémon that actually fainted; a Pokémon still alive when the raid ends is not treated as if it fainted.
  • Average on-field DPS is damage divided by active field time and excludes time spent on the bench after a hot swap. Peak DPS starts at zero energy immediately before an undodged boss charged hit. The attacker keeps using fast moves while that boss move repeats back-to-back; total damage and elapsed time are measured through the attacker’s first charged move. If it faints first, the scenario is unavailable.
  • The displayed full-HP fast-only and charged-only limits are simple no-dodge comparisons. The typical mixed hit counts come from full simulations using the selected dodge and player strategies. Hidden Power is tested across its 16 possible types.
  • Battle DPS includes fainting, switching, relobby downtime, and the selected dodge and player strategies.
  • The selected weather and friendship bonuses apply to every simulated battle. Party Power, Adventure Effects, and Purified Gems remain off.
  • Exclude legacy removes fast and charged moves marked Elite or legacy; currently available signature and special-form moves remain eligible.
  • A fast analytical pass retains the strongest candidate movesets for full simulations; all displayed scores come from the raid simulator.
  • Mega rows show standardized per-attacker potential. Six copies of the same Mega are a ranking abstraction, not a legal raid party.
  • An asterisk (*) marks an Elite or legacy move.
