Controls & practice rules

Tap Fast attack or hold F / Space to keep attacking. C uses your charged move; D dodges. Press 1–6 to switch, and P to pause. One action can be queued during a move; a new input replaces it. An attack cannot be cancelled halfway through.

The battle advances in 0.5-second steps using the simulator’s manual battle rules, including its dodge window. This is a practice model, not an exact recreation of the Pokémon GO app. Choose a surviving slot after a faint, or rejoin after your whole team faints. Switching tabs pauses practice.

Attempts stay in this page until you leave or reload. Export a replay to keep it and open it in the simulator’s Battle replay tab.
