Controls & practice rules

Tap the battle screen for a fast move. Swipe left, right, up or down to dodge; dragging with a mouse also works. Buttons keep their own actions. Tap the circular charged-move button when it fills. F / Space, C and D also work; 1–6 switches teammates and P pauses.

After a faint, wait 2 seconds while the first surviving team slot enters automatically. A full team wipe shows “All your Pokémon have fainted” for those 2 seconds, then opens the lobby. A phantom relobby shows the same screen for 2 seconds; your team is already healed. Wait out the rejoin delay, then press Rejoin raid. Team slots stay on screen throughout the battle.

Stable controls keep one action queued until your Pokémon is ready. Dodge is available while a boss attack is incoming. Training mode pauses when switching tabs. Realistic mode keeps the clock running and disables pauses, slow motion and automatic fast attacks. Export a replay before leaving or reloading to keep your attempt.
