Controls & practice rules

Tap the battle screen for a fast move. Swipe left, right, up or down to dodge; dragging with a mouse also works. Buttons keep their own actions. Tap the circular charged-move button when it fills. F / Space, C and D also work; 1–6 switches teammates and P pauses.

After a faint, wait 2 seconds while the first surviving team slot enters automatically. A full team wipe sends you to the lobby. Wait out the rejoin delay, then press Rejoin raid. Team slots stay on screen throughout the battle.

One action can be queued during a move. The battle uses the simulator’s half-second manual timing and dodge window. Switching tabs pauses practice. Export a replay before leaving or reloading to keep your attempt.
